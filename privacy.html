Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Short version: EduPrompt does not collect your name, email, or the texts you analyse. Everything you type stays on your device. We use Google Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate.

1. Who we are

EduPrompt is a free grammar analysis and teleprompter tool for English teachers and learners, developed by Ravel Robaina. The app is accessible at ravelrobaina.github.io/tools/eduprompt.html and on Google Play.

2. What we collect

EduPrompt collects minimal data. Specifically:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — We use GA4 to understand how people use the app. GA4 automatically collects: device type and operating system, browser type and language, approximate geographic location (country/city level), pages visited and time spent, and general usage events (e.g. session start). This data is anonymous and never linked to your identity.
  • Local Storage — The app saves your preferences (font size, speed, active filters, last text) in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifier
  • The texts you paste or type into the analyser
  • Account credentials (there are no accounts)
  • Payment information
  • Location data beyond country/city level (via GA4)

4. How we use the data

Analytics data collected via GA4 is used exclusively to understand which features are used, identify performance issues, and improve the product. We do not sell, trade, or share this data with third parties beyond the analytics service itself.

5. Third-party services

Google Analytics 4 — Operated by Google LLC. Data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of GA tracking at any time using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or a browser extension such as uBlock Origin.

EduPrompt does not use any other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Data retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months (GA4 default). Local Storage data remains on your device until you clear your browser data or uninstall the app.

7. Children's privacy

EduPrompt does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains no accounts, no messaging, and no data upload — there is nothing children can submit. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Your rights

Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, there is typically no personal data for us to retrieve, correct, or delete on your behalf. For any concerns about GA4 data, you may use Google's opt-out tool linked above.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you may have additional rights under GDPR. You can exercise these by contacting us directly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of EduPrompt after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
